# GFCRI Personal Overseas Growth Plan

Date: 2026-07-11
Scope: overseas personal subscription soft launch and paid beta.

## 1. Positioning

GFCRI Personal Pro should be marketed as a macro-risk monitoring subscription, not as an investment-advice product.

Core positioning:

```text
Track whether global macro-financial risk is heating up, what is driving it, and what to watch next.
```

Short tagline options:

- See macro risk before it dominates headlines.
- A daily risk brief for global macro investors.
- Track systemic risk, hidden pressure, and transmission channels.
- Know what is driving macro stress today.

Avoid:

- Buy/sell signal.
- Crisis prediction.
- Guaranteed early warning.
- Portfolio allocation advice.
- Trade recommendation.

Required footer language:

```text
GFCRI is for informational and risk-monitoring purposes only. It is not investment advice, trading advice, asset-allocation advice, or a recommendation to buy or sell any financial product.
```
